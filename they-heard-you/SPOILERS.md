# Walkthrough

## The Escape, Step By Step

The NEXT line under your pockets always says what to do. Every tweaker hands you one piece of the way out. Whenever you hear running footsteps, click Your seat and then Pretend restrained.

1. **The spoon.** Lift the seat cushion and take the spoon. Hold it and click the floor fitting. It moves a little, then the spoon folds in half with a CLANK.
2. **Cletus's first visit.** That noise brings Cletus. You get extra warning this time: get on the couch and Pretend restrained. He rants, fiddles with the fitting, and leaves his screwdriver in the kitchen drawer. (If you never touch the fitting, he comes anyway at about 90 seconds.)
3. **Off the floor.** Open the kitchen drawer, take the screwdriver, and crank the fitting twice more. The chain comes off the floor; the cuff stays on. Now you can reach the dinette and the back room.
4. **Bobby pins.** At the dinette, grab the emergency ashtray for bobby pins and a cigarette butt. In the back room, the army footlocker at the foot of the bunk is padlocked, but your hands shake too hard to pick it.
5. **Darlene.** About 30 seconds after you're free, she rampages through looking for her lighter, with it in her fist the whole time. She throws it on the floor by the couch. Grab it.
6. **Steady hands.** Smoke the butt with the lighter: 60 seconds of steady hands. Get to the back room fast and use the bobby pins on the footlocker. Inside are the axe, the paperback, and a note: KEY STAYS ON CLETUS. ALWAYS. Take the axe. (Ran out of time? The ashtray always has another butt.)
7. **TV night.** Cletus puts you on the news, laughs at you, and dangles the cuff key on its shoelace.
8. **The dance.** Next he comes back in Darlene's nightgown and dances. Mid-spin the shoelace snaps and the key skitters to your feet. Press **GRAB THE KEY** (or Space). If you miss it, he takes it back and dances again later.
9. **The cuff.** Hold the key and click the floor fitting or the cuff.
10. **Dale.** The door is plywood over steel bracing; sober, the axe just bounces off. Dale comes in, plays Fine Again, and offers his bulb. Hit it (press 1). If you pass, he comes back in about a minute and a half.
11. **Out.** High, every swing counts double: three swings and you're through. Click every shadow before it reaches you, and wrap the axe in the rag to keep it quiet. DITCH WATER.

The back-room hatch is glued and taped shut. It is not an exit.

## How to play

Click things for their menu (keys 1–5 pick an option). Click an item in your pockets to pick it up and see what it can do.

## Tricks

- Crank the fitting in bursts. Noise over 70 brings someone. Better still, wrap the rag around the screwdriver first.
- Peek through the blinds before doing anything loud.
- Once you're free, throw the paperback out the window and you get over a minute of cover while Cletus reads it aloud.
- Don't leave puke on the floor. Wipe it with the rag.
- Keep the used needles for the moment someone grabs you.

## Visits

There's no countdown. When you hear running footsteps, click Your seat and then Pretend restrained. You have about 2.5 seconds. If you're anywhere else, or the loose fitting is in plain view, they grab you and beat you unconscious. You wake up chained with your gear bundled in the kitchen drawer. The second time, they kill you, and you can wake up again from the last time you came to.

Darlene never talks with you. She tears the kitchen apart looking for her lighter, screams in your face and rips open the trash bag in the aisle, with the lighter in her fist the whole time. Then she finds it and slams it on the floor by the couch. Grab it. If she doesn't have it, she drops or steals something else on her way out.

## Dale

Dale comes in once the cuff is off (and again if you pass on the bulb). Be on the couch like any visit. He sits beside you, plays Fine Again on his radio, cries about his amp, smokes his bulb, then offers it (press 1 to hit it, 2 to pass). Either way he leaves the radio playing on the floor. Leave it on: it covers half the noise of cranking the fitting.

Hitting the bulb gives about 2 minutes of tweaker strength: the fitting comes loose in two cranks, and the door goes in two swings. The price is shadow people. Click every one you see before it reaches you; one that gets to you makes you scream, and two screams bring someone. Watch the PARANOIA meter. At 100 you lose it and scream anyway. Footsteps with a question mark ("[Outside?]") aren't real.

Have the axe ready (and wrapped in the rag), take the hit, stare down the first shadow, then chop the door three times.

## Fire

Hold the lighter and click something that burns: the floor papers by the couch (even while chained), the trash bag in the aisle, or either pizza box once you're free. It grows for a few seconds, Darlene smells the smoke, and Cletus charges in with a bucket of dishwater. He puts it out and beats you unconscious wherever you are, even on the couch, then takes the lighter back to Darlene. A fire never kills you, even after an earlier knockout. The used needles still work on him when he comes at you. Burnt things stay burnt.

## Cletus Inspection

Choose Tell me more to keep Cletus talking. On the fifth continuation he approaches for a brief animated kiss, then returns to the conversation. Skip bypasses the animation. Enough ends the visit and resolves any inspection evidence normally.

Once you've opened the footlocker, Cletus comes in, turns the TV on and makes you watch yourself on the news (LOCAL MAN STILL MISSING) while he laughs at you. Be on the couch when he arrives, or you get beaten first. He leaves it on Channel 4 with the sound up: the news, a commercial break (Zwinkys Malt Liquor), and back to the news. Head to the dinette or the rear bunk to hear it properly. Once you're free, the dinette lets you turn it off or mute it with the buttons on the set. Changing channels needs the remote.

After TV night, Cletus comes in dressed in Darlene's nightgown and dances for twenty seconds to Goodbye Horses. You have to watch, and that's when the key falls off his neck.

Ronnie lies on the couch in the back room. Rapid moves near him or noisy hatch shoving can startle him into an alarm. Stop moving and let his agitation settle. Routine visits only start after Cletus's first visit.

After a beating, open the kitchen drawer and Use the belongings bundle to get everything back, the axe and keys included, then loosen the fitting again with the screwdriver.

## Drawer And Returns

1. At Kitchenette, Use the drawer front to open it.
2. Grab the rag from inside.
3. To return an item, choose Put back, select it, and click where it came from while that place is open.
